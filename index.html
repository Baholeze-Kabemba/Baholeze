Profile

Understand the systems to secure them better.

Baholeze Kabemba works at the intersection of cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure and artificial intelligence — approaching each discipline as part of the same underlying problem: understanding how systems actually behave.

On the security side, this means building SOC simulations, analyzing logs and practicing detection and investigation workflows. On the infrastructure side, it means designing environments across OpenStack, VMware ESXi and Linux, and automating them with tools like Ansible, Docker and Podman.

More recently, that same systems-first mindset has extended into local AI — running large language models with Ollama, building RAG pipelines, and exploring how AI can support security operations rather than replace the analyst behind them.

The throughline is practical experimentation: building real labs, breaking them, and learning from how they fail.
